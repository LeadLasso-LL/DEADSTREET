# The Kurgan Group ? regular unit design
Updated 2026-09-12.

All five units are Russian. Worn military clothing, mismatched surplus, faded camouflage and practical armor; normal accepted human proportions, modest garment thickness. Patches depict the established black/red circular Kurgan tiger emblem, simplified at pixel sleeve scale.

## Approved wardrobe
- Pistol: faded olive field cap; sandy blonde hair and rough stubble; black aviators; partly open khaki shirt over navy/white stripes, sleeves rolled to elbows; dark olive trousers; black combat boots; brown webbing belt, holster and utility pouches; upper-sleeve patch.
- SMG: charcoal beanie over olive ski mask; faded dark green sweatshirt pushed to forearms; brown canvas chest rig with emblem; tan trousers with olive knees; brown combat boots; black fingerless gloves.
- Shotgun: scratched plain rounded olive helmet; dark brown hair and thick untrimmed beard; faded woodland jacket; worn olive ballistic vest with shell loops; dark brown trousers; black lace-up boots; tan work gloves; sleeve emblem.
- Rifle: green camouflage-covered helmet and black chinstrap; concealed brown hair, clean-shaven; olive lower-face gaiter; green/brown camouflage shirt and trousers; weathered tan carrier with olive magazine pouches; dark green knees; brown boots and olive gloves; sleeve emblem.
- Sniper: khaki hood; concealed reddish hair and scruffy ginger beard; olive face cloth; brown/khaki mountain smock with dark olive shoulder and elbow panels; sparse faded camouflage strips at hood/shoulders; low-profile olive harness; brown trousers with khaki knees; muddy brown boots and olive gloves; sleeve emblem below camouflage strips.

## Production scope
Code-native outfit profile and review renders using the existing rig and weapon handling. Does not yet bind production gameplay atlases or introduce special-unit mechanics. Specials remain deferred until regular faction wardrobes are complete.

## Verification
Full review: 1,080 generated/rendered frames; accepted body geometry retained and no render/bounds failures. Includes eight facings, standing/aiming, motion samples and armory combinations. Standing shoulder joins, silhouettes and weapon proportions visually reviewed. SMG emblem placement corrected onto the canvas rig before final render.
