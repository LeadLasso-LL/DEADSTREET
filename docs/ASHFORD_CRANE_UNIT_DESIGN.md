# The Ashford-Crane Collective - regular unit design
Updated 2026-09-12.

Every unit wears a cheap ivory molded-plastic theatrical mask fully covering the face, black elastic securing strap, dark eyeholes and light scuffs. Five different sculpted expressions. Accepted ordinary human proportions remain unchanged. No special units in this pass.

## Approved outfits
- Pistol: small crooked smirk; chestnut brown hair swept back to collar; faded burgundy short-sleeve open-collar button-up; charcoal pleated trousers; black leather loafers; thin black gloves; silver wristwatch.
- SMG: wide exaggerated grin; messy bleached blonde hair with dark roots; faded white ribbed tank; scattered black arm tattoos; dark olive cargo pants; black-and-white high-top sneakers; black fingerless gloves; compact black sling bag against the back.
- Shotgun: deep theatrical frown and raised inner eyebrows; short dark hair concealed beneath washed-out plum pullover hood; faded gray straight-leg jeans; scuffed black work boots; black leather gloves; modest hoodie thickness.
- Rifle: angry scowl and sharply lowered eyebrows; short black hair neatly combed back; slate-blue quarter-zip over black undershirt; charcoal utility trousers; black belt with compact magazine pouches; black lace-up boots; fitted black gloves.
- Sniper: blank expressionless mask; black hood forward around mask, hair concealed; matte black hip-length rain jacket zipped high; reinforced black cargo pants; black lace-up boots; thin black gloves; small black canvas hip equipment bag.
