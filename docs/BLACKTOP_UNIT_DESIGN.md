# Blacktop Apostles MC - regular unit design
Updated 2026-09-12.

Every unit's outer leather garment carries the approved hooded-skeleton/cracked-halo emblem and worn BLACKTOP APOSTLES / MC patches, with dirty ivory and burnt-orange chest patches.
