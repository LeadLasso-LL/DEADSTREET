# Dead Street unit art standard

Owner direction, 2026-09-11. Applies to every faction and weapon combination.

- Preserve the established normal body proportions, joint positions, and torso,
  head, hip, and leg relationship. A clothing request does not authorize changing
  anatomy. Different builds require explicit character direction, such as a Brute.
- Show a looser garment through restrained folds or wrinkles. Omit the bagginess
  if necessary; never inflate, shrink, stretch, or detach the underlying body.
- For every new unit, visually inspect the **left shoulder in the standing image**
  and explicitly confirm it joins the torso. Check both shoulders. No detached
  sleeve caps or heavy black interior outline dividing an arm from the body.
- Keep each weapon's identity and reasonable dimensions relative to the same body
  throughout facings and stances. Natural perspective and occlusion are allowed;
  unexplained miniature or oversized versions are not. Hands follow actual grips.
- Compare with an existing accepted unit on the same canvas at the same scale.
  Inspect the hip/hem overlap, shoulders, head, limbs, and weapon both enlarged and
  at gameplay scale. Do not independently resize each preview to fit its cell.
- Before full unit production is accepted, review all eight directions and every
  produced stance, including movement, aiming, firing, reload, cover and wounds.
  Inspect the relevant weapon combinations; one good frame is not blanket proof.
- The technical lead must reject obvious visual faults before presenting work.
  Frame-clearance checks and geometry equality cannot certify visual quality.
  Describe the views actually inspected; never claim unrendered poses were checked.
