## Causes and changes

- Target selection repeatedly validated every source/candidate pair and checked
  LOS before cheaper ordering rules. Eligible rows are prepared once; assault
  and sniper checks follow the existing priority/distance rules with identical
  tie behavior and live eligibility.
- Weapon/tier/role profiles, vehicle transforms, sorted vehicle IDs, readiness
  and vehicle topology strings were repeatedly reconstructed. Synchronous scopes
  reuse stable data and restore the preceding scope before the next input/update.
  Death, damage, orders and other live combat state remain live.
- Navigation connected each new endpoint to a large visibility graph. Known cover
  endpoints are prepared before combat. Conservative rectangle hierarchies prune
  obstacle checks; a legal seed route bounds endpoint candidates, with a full
  fallback. Exact slab predicates and route behavior are retained. Reachability
  reuses existing connectivity and prepared endpoints.
- Cover ranking duplicated protection work and checked LOS before rejecting
  slots for unchanged range/progress rules. Those cheap rejections now happen
  first; standalone evaluation remains complete.
- LOS considered many obstacles in a segment's broad bounding rectangle.
  A conservative ray hierarchy narrows candidates, retaining exact nearest-hit,
  interior-edge and obstacle-ID tie rules. Its live shape snapshot is checked
  once per synchronous scope, including direct edits between scopes.
- LOS and reachability memoization grew without bound as moving endpoints changed.
  Two generations now cap retained entries at 16,384 LOS and 8,192 reachability
  rows, retain useful recent queries and clear both generations on topology edits.
- First-wound anchor JSON and clothing-mask loading caused presentation stalls.
  Selected actors' assets are prepared during setup. Shot history is indexed once
  per actor refresh instead of rescanned per actor. Art and animation assets are
  unchanged.

A native 32-unit presentation profile at an intermediate stage attributed about
14.2 ms/update to simulation and about 2.5 ms/frame to actor presentation plus
dynamic drawing. Profiling numbers include measurement overhead. The remaining
limit is substantially simulation work, not sprite count alone.

Preparation moves work into setup; it is not free. The later runs record

28.57 s (32 Bulwarks). These include scene/asset loading
and the one-second fixture wait, not solely navigation preparation.
